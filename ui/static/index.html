How does the fidelity budget of a simulator decide whether a policy transfers? One policy is calibrated in a reference simulator, then replayed, unchanged, as five physical knobs are degraded one at a time — control period, ground friction, contact rigidity, sensor noise, and observation delay. Each knob is a budget you can spend or save; this dashboard maps exactly where reliability collapses.

Headline: only sensor noise fails gracefully (wide cliff — reliability erodes over a long budget range and never fully collapses), while dt, mu, k and delay all fail at a critical point (narrow cliff — one small step below the safe budget and the policy stops transferring entirely). The failure mode says which knob broke: escape = the block cannot be stopped (friction), timeout = the push cannot finish (dt / k / delay).
